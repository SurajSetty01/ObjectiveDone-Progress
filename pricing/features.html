Why this matters for pricing: only four things scale with how hard a member uses the app — transcription minutes, AI questions, card scans and stored media. Everything else is either free or a flat monthly platform cost. The plans on the next pages are built around exactly those four.
Two findings worth acting on now.
• Google Wallet passes cannot ship for an India-registered company. Google's own support documentation says the Wallet API "isn't available to new businesses in India". Apple Wallet is unaffected, so the plan is iPhone-only Wallet passes and an ordinary home-screen shortcut on Android.
• Delivering the sign-in code over WhatsApp is cheaper than SMS once volume passes roughly 4,300 codes a month — ₹0.12 a message against ₹0.20 — but it carries a fixed monthly platform fee, so SMS stays the right default until then.
