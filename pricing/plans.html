The rules that keep costs honest

GuardWhy it exists
Hard spending cap per providerRequired by the contract (A19). The account cannot run away in a bad month, whatever a member does.
Card reading happens on the phone firstKeeps the most-used capture feature at zero marginal cost; the cloud is only a fallback for cards the phone cannot read.
Fast model by defaultEveryday "who did I meet" questions do not need the expensive model. Escalation is a Pro feature, not a default.
Transcription runs on our server, never in the appThe provider key never ships inside the app, so it cannot be extracted and spent by someone else.
Allowances do not roll overStops a year of unused minutes landing in one very expensive month.
Three things to settle before these go live.
• The prices assume the fast concierge model. Running everything on the smarter one takes Pro's worst case from ₹379 to about ₹850 — more than the plan costs once the September price rise lands. Escalation has to stay selective, not default.
• They also assume recordings live on Cloudflare R2. Leaving them on the default storage adds roughly ₹42 per Pro member per month at scale, cutting the margin by 8 points.
• Every rupee here rests on ₹96 to the dollar. A 10% currency move shifts Pro's cost to serve by about ₹38.
