Free and Premium — as shipped.

This is the plan the app actually enforces, not a proposal. One paid tier, two terms: ₹400 a month or ₹4,000 a year — twelve months for the price of ten. The earlier Basic/Pro split was dropped: two paid tiers meant two allowance tables, two upgrade prompts and a decision the member has to make before they understand the product. Costs below use the measured rates from the previous page.

No monthly unit allowances — metered per day server-side, and fair-use capped rather than rationed
Why the free tier gives one minute of recording and not ten. The cost of a recording is not what is being priced. A minute of audio is ~0.5 MB — about ₹0.0007 a month to store — and transcribing it costs ₹0.05. Storage and inference together are a rounding error. The limit exists because it is legible: "one minute free" is a rule a member understands the first time they hit it, and it lands during a real conversation rather than buried in a settings screen. An AI-token quota is cheaper to give away and nobody can feel it, which makes it worthless as an upgrade prompt. The trade, accepted knowingly: free users hit this on their first genuine meeting. That is the intent, not a side effect. The recording is always saved at the cap — losing someone's words to a paywall would be indefensible.

The rules that keep costs honest

GuardWhy it exists
Hard spending cap per providerRequired by the contract (A19). The account cannot run away in a bad month, whatever a member does.
Card reading happens on the phone firstKeeps the most-used capture feature at zero marginal cost; the cloud is only a fallback for cards the phone cannot read.
Fast model by defaultEveryday "who did I meet" questions do not need the expensive model. Escalation is a Pro feature, not a default.
Transcription runs on our server, never in the appThe provider key never ships inside the app, so it cannot be extracted and spent by someone else.
Allowances do not roll overStops a year of unused minutes landing in one very expensive month.
Three things to settle before these go live.
• The prices assume the fast concierge model. Running everything on the smarter one takes Pro's worst case from ₹379 to about ₹850 — more than the plan costs once the September price rise lands. Escalation has to stay selective, not default.
• They also assume recordings live on Cloudflare R2. Leaving them on the default storage adds roughly ₹42 per Pro member per month at scale, cutting the margin by 8 points.
• Every rupee here rests on ₹96 to the dollar. A 10% currency move shifts Pro's cost to serve by about ₹38.
