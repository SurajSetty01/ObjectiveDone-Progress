Running costs & member plans

What ObjectiveDone costs to run —
and what a member should pay.

Three pages, in order: which features actually cost money and what runs them, what each single use costs at the vendors' published rates, and the two member plans those numbers support. Every price here is a real published figure with its source linked — nothing is estimated except where it says so.

Only four things scale with usage

everything else is free or a flat monthly cost

🎙️ Transcribing a voice note

₹0.29
per minute of audio

A member who records ten 3-minute conversations a month costs about ₹9 in transcription.

💬 One question to the concierge

₹0.31
per question & answer

Cheap individually, but the thing members will use most. 100 questions a month ≈ ₹31.

📇 Reading a paper card

₹0.00
per scan, on the phone

Free when the phone does the reading. A cloud fallback for difficult cards costs ₹0.14 each, and the first 1,000 a month are free.

🗄️ Keeping photos & recordings

₹1.44
per GB per month

On Cloudflare R2, which charges nothing to send files back. On the default storage the same GB costs ₹2.05 plus ₹8.65 every time it is downloaded.

Converted at ₹96 to the US dollar — the assumption used throughout this section, and one that moves. Full working, per-vendor rates and sources on the costs page.

How the plans work

two tiers, built from those four numbers

Basic

For the member who swaps cards and keeps a tidy contact book.

₹299/ member / month

Costs at most ₹72 to serve — a 76% margin even if fully used.

  • ✓Unlimited cards, QR, NFC and sharing
  • ✓Unlimited contacts, tags and imports
  • ✓60 minutes of voice-note transcription
  • ✓150 concierge questions
  • ✓2 GB of recordings & photos
Recommended

Pro

For the founder who records every meeting and asks the concierge daily.

₹799/ member / month

Costs at most ₹379 to serve — a 53% margin even if fully used.

  • ✓Everything in Basic
  • ✓300 minutes of transcription
  • ✓750 concierge questions
  • ✓20 GB of recordings & photos
  • ✓Summaries & action items on every recording

Allowances, the cost behind each one, and the margin they leave are broken down on the plans page.

A note on how these were built: the plan prices are set so that a member using their entire allowance still costs meaningfully less than they pay — the arithmetic is shown on the plans page rather than hidden. Members who barely use AI cost almost nothing to serve, which is what funds the heavy users.
Three decisions this research forced into the open:
• Google Wallet passes cannot be issued by an India-registered company — Google's own documentation closes the API to new Indian businesses. Wallet ships on iPhone only.
• Where recordings and photos live decides the infrastructure bill. At 5,000 members it is the difference between ₹47,735 and ₹6,193 a month, and it is much easier to choose now than to migrate later.
• Which model answers concierge questions triples the cost of the feature members touch most. All three are worked through on the costs page.
