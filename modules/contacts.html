Module 08

Contacts

In progress

Everyone you meet lands in one searchable rolodex — tagged, sorted, and a swipe away. Each person gets a full page of their own: details, where you met, written memories, voice-memo rows, and a one-tap jump to ask the concierge about them. The whole rolodex now lives in the cloud, private to your account, so it survives the phone it was typed on.

App screen Action Decision Cloud / backend Outcome / milestone Pending — later milestone
01

Find anyone

Built · live on the cloud

Search, sort and filter by tag; a swipe on a row deletes or tags them, and every row is read and saved in the cloud. The list itself got a quieter, cleaner face today: where a contact came from is now a small mark on their photo instead of a labelled row, how long ago you met sits softly at the right ("2w", "3mo"), the dividers between rows are clearer, and a scanned contact's photo appears instantly once the app has seen it before.

02

A contact's page

Built · live on the cloud

One page per person — details, a FOLLOW-UP section, memories, voice notes and where you met. Every edit saves straight to the cloud. Memories, voice and tags now live together in one section: written memories sit on a dated timeline, the voice-note recorder is right beside them, and the tag sheet is one tap away — the full story is feature 05 below. The follow-up section sets a reminder against this person in two taps, and snoozes or completes it later; the phone is nudged when one falls due, even with the app closed — the deeper story is on the Home page.

03

A voice memo, end to end

Built · verified on the phone

Recording is free and nothing transcribes by itself — a transcript spends a paid model call, so it is a deliberate step. Each memo row offers "Generate transcript"; the transcript comes back with a one-paragraph summary and the commitments made out loud. After a failure the same button reads "Try again". Delete the transcript only, the recording only, or both — a memo with neither part removes itself.

04

Add someone

Built · Google import pending

Two doors in: type a person yourself, or bring a whole list from the phone book, a CSV or a vCard — every import passes a duplicate guard first, then lands in the cloud rolodex.

05

Memories pinned to a day, tags that don't multiply

Built · live on the cloud

A memory can now carry its date: pick the calendar day it belongs to and it takes its place on the contact's timeline, newest first, editable and deletable like any entry. The voice-note recorder sits right beside the written memory — no detour to record — and tags are one tap away in the same section. The tags themselves stay tidy: start typing "10xn" and the "10xnetworking" you already have offers itself in both tag sheets instead of a fresh duplicate, and a tag that differs only in capital letters can no longer be created twice.

A

Where the rolodex lives

Live on the cloud

Every contact screen talks through one data door, and that door opens onto the cloud — people, tags, favourites and follow-up reminders are rows only your account can read, and photos and memo audio upload to storage only your account can reach. A transcript is made in the cloud when you ask for one, and lands back on the memo with its summary and action items.

S

Voice memo lifecycle

Built · verified on the phone

The recorder's real lifecycle, with real sound. The microphone had a defect where it died app-wide after one take; it now recovers on its own, and back-to-back recordings are verified on the device. The transcript is a separate, deliberate step — asked for per memo, retried after a failure, deletable on its own.

T

What powers this module

Feature by feature — what each moving part actually runs on.

  • List & filtersSearch, name/newest sort and tag-chip filters backed by the contact_tags registry · swipe a row left to delete, right to tag · redesigned 4 Aug: origin shows as a small mark on the photo, met-ago sits quietly at the right, dividers are clearer, and scanned photos are cached on the phone so they appear instantly after first sight
  • Reusable tagsBoth tag sheets look the typed text up against the tags you already have and offer the match — "10xn" surfaces "10xnetworking" — and the registry treats capitals as the same tag, so a case-only duplicate can no longer be created
  • Dated memoriesA date picker pins each written memory to the calendar day it belongs to · entries save with the contact in the cloud and render as a timeline on the page, newest first, each one editable and deletable
  • Shared grouped fieldsThe create/edit form reuses the Card Builder's FieldGroupsEditor — one grouped-fields component across both modules · every exit from the form now routes through leaveScreen(), the fix for the native save-crash found and closed on 27 July
  • Where-we-met@react-native-community/geolocation stamps coordinates plus free-text place · opens in Maps from the contact page
  • ImportsPhone book via react-native-contacts (runtime permission both platforms) · CSV / vCard via @react-native-documents/picker with in-app parsers · duplicate guard skips people already saved
  • Voice memosreact-native-audio-recorder-player, patched at source for a defect that killed the microphone app-wide after one take, plus a self-healing retry · recording duration measured by wall clock, so a quick second take is no longer rejected as too short · verified back-to-back on the device · the recorder now sits directly on the contact page beside the written memory, and these memos also appear in the Meeting Recorder's all-app history
  • TranscriptionThe transcribe-memo edge function on Cloudflare Workers AI — Whisper large v3 turbo turns speech into text; llama-3.3-70b writes the one-paragraph summary and pulls out action items · runs per memo, on request, keys stay server-side
  • Follow-up remindersThe reminders table from migration 0011 with owner-only security on every verb · a pg_cron job sweeps every five minutes and sends the reminder.due push · set/snooze/complete from this page's FOLLOW-UP section or from Home
  • Where the data livesLive on Supabase PostgreSQL — the contacts and contact_tags tables plus the tags, met_location and recordings columns from migration 0004, applied and verified 27 July · owner-only row security, proven with two separate signed-in sessions
  • Photos & memo audioBoth upload to the card-media bucket on Cloudflare R2 under owner-scoped rules, so media follows the account — a memo recorded on one phone plays back on another
  • Ask the conciergeThe deployed ai-chat cloud function answers on Cloudflare Workers AI (llama-3.3-70b), grounded only in your own contacts, notes and memo transcripts · a paid Anthropic Claude key upgrades answer quality with no code change
