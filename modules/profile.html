Module 12

Profile & Account

In progress

Your name, photo and company details live in one place, next to your data, your invites and the door out. Edit them once and every new card starts from the right information — cards you have already made stay exactly as you designed them.

App screen Action Decision Cloud / backend Outcome / milestone Pending — later milestone
01

The hub

Built

Your photo in the top corner opens the hub — export, invites, support and the door out, with every row doing exactly what it says. Sign out now lives only here, behind an are-you-sure confirm — the temporary sign-out button Home carried during development was removed on 1 August. The FAQ and legal rows open tidy coming-soon pages until the copy is written.

02

Account details

Built

One identity form feeds every card you make next — photo changes save on the spot, text fields wait for an explicit Save so half-typed details are never kept.

03

Activity Log · App Usage · Credit Usage

Built · runs on live data

Three real settings pages now sit under the account block. The Activity Log lists your sign-ins, sign-outs, phone moves and follow-up events, read through a sealed account record built so it can never show an IP address. App Usage shows the time you spend in the app as a fourteen-day trend — counted on your phone only and never uploaded anywhere. Credit Usage, for Premium members, shows what each day's AI features used, worded so a change of provider never reshapes your history. The Premium plan page itself now leads with the ObjectiveDone mark on the same glossy gold card — gold gradient, white lettering — that the Home pill and the Profile card wear.

A

Where your details live

Live · pictures upload too

Your details save to your account in the cloud, locked to you alone, and come back on whichever phone you sign in on — and the picture files now travel with them instead of staying on one handset.

T

What powers this module

Feature by feature — what each moving part actually runs on.

  • Profile hubReact Native 0.80 (New Architecture) + TypeScript · React Navigation native-stack · rows for account details, data export, invites, support and sign out
  • CSV exportContacts serialised to CSV in-app, then handed to the native share sheet via React Native's Share API — mail, Drive and Notes all accept it
  • Invite friendsReady-made invite message through the same native Share API sheet
  • Contact supportLinking opens a pre-addressed mailto with the subject line already filled in
  • Sign outConfirm dialog → Supabase session ends, every local store wiped · the app's only sign-out since Home's development-time button was removed on 1 August
  • Identity formreact-hook-form + zod (blur-validated) · TanStack Query · avatar picked with react-native-image-picker and saved instantly · fields release the keyboard before the screen closes, which is what fixed the native save crash found on 27 July
  • Company websiteSaved with your identity and handed to the card builder's logo finder, which reads the site's own artwork. The opt-in checkbox that used to gate this was removed on 31 July — a setting you have to find before a feature works is a setting that stops it working
  • Profile storageLIVE on the Supabase PostgreSQL profiles table since 27 July, via supabaseProfileRepository — every row is locked to its owner by database security rules, proven with two separate signed-in sessions. The on-phone MMKV version is now only a fallback for offline development
  • Picture filesYour photo and any company logo upload for real to Cloudflare R2 (via short-lived presigned URLs from the media-sign edge function) and survive a reinstall · signing out clears the cached copies so the next account never sees the last one's pictures
  • One phone at a timeprofiles.current_device_id — signing in on a new phone claims the account and signs the previous one out. Active since 27 July
