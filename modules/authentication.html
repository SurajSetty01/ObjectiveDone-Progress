Module 02

Authentication

In progress

Getting in takes an email and thirty seconds — a six-digit code in a branded email, or a password for returning members, live on the cloud today. The one-phone-per-account lock is now active: signing in on a new phone quietly retires the old one, so two people can never share an account. Email is the only channel for now — the client's call. The SMS path is built and kept switched off until his provider paperwork comes through; Apple and Google sign-in and WhatsApp codes join later.

P

Things to do

Four items, and every one waits on an outside account or approval rather than on code. Signing in by email — one-time code or password — is the live path today, and email-only is the client's own call.

Integrations 4 items

  • Google sign-in blocked on a signing keyThe app side is written and waiting. What it needs is a Google Cloud sign-in client registered against the RELEASE signing key — and the release build is still signed with a development key, so that key does not exist yet. Until it does, the button is hidden rather than shown and failing.
  • Codes by SMS built · switched offThe flow itself is finished and sits dormant in the app — the client chose to launch with email only. Switching it on waits on him: his SmartPing account will carry the codes through a small server hook, and what we need is his approved message template (the telecom paperwork known as DLT) and the API details. That approval has a queue in front of it, so it is the thing to start first.
  • Codes over WhatsAppA separate and much longer path: it needs a WhatsApp Business account and a message template approved by Meta. Recommended after launch, not before.
  • [iPhone] Sign in with AppleNeeds an Apple Developer account and a Mac to configure. The sign-in seam already accepts additional providers.
