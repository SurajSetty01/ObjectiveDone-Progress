Module 09

AI Concierge

In progress

A private concierge you chat with about the people you meet — every conversation keeps its own thread, with a drawer to search, pin and rename them. Your chats live in your own cloud account, and the concierge now answers for real, on the club's own AI allowance — grounded in everything you have stored: your contacts, notes, dated memories, every memo's title, summary and spoken commitments, and the Meeting Recorder's transcripts. It opens in about two seconds, your conversation already remembered. When your notes hold no answer, it says so plainly instead of inventing one. A paid Claude key remains a one-line upgrade to answer quality, not a prerequisite.

A

How the concierge is wired

Live · answering on the club's own allowance

The chat plugs into one switchboard — your threads and messages are kept in the cloud, and the concierge's service reads everything you have stored before it answers: your contacts, notes, dated memories, every memo's title, summary and spoken commitments, and the Meeting Recorder's transcripts — always only your own. It opens in about two seconds because your conversation is remembered and fetched ahead. The answering model runs on the club's own AI allowance — the same one that reads cards and transcribes memos, so no second vendor and no new bill. A paid Claude key upgrades answer quality without touching the app.

T

What powers this module

Feature by feature — what each moving part actually runs on.

  • Threaded chat experienceReact Native 0.80 (New Architecture) + TypeScript · thread drawer with search, pin, rename, delete · a new chat auto-titles itself from your first message — all built
  • Chat dataLIVE on Supabase PostgreSQL — ai_threads + ai_messages (migration 0004, applied and verified 2026-07-27) · the app now runs on supabaseAiRepository, so threads and messages persist to the account rather than the phone
  • Chat privacyOwner-only row-level security on both tables and, unlike cards or contacts, no administrator read path at all · isolation proven with two live signed-in sessions
  • Concierge serviceSupabase Edge Function ai-chat — deployed and ANSWERING · verifies a genuine signed-in user (an anon key is rejected) and reads the caller's own contacts, notes, dated memories, every memo's title, summary and spoken commitments, and the Meeting Recorder's transcripts under their own credentials to ground the answer, replaying the recent turns of the thread · it previously received only raw memo transcripts, which is why saved memories were ignored · the chat now opens in about two seconds — the conversation is remembered and fetched ahead
  • Answering modelllama-3.3-70b on Cloudflare Workers AI — the club's existing allowance, the same vendor that reads cards and transcribes memos, so no new account and no new bill · instructed to answer only from the member's own data and to refuse to invent — verified by asking for an email the notes do not hold; it said so plainly
  • Claude upgrade pathSetting ANTHROPIC_API_KEY as a Supabase secret switches ai-chat to Anthropic Claude in place — no app change, no rebuild · an answer-quality upgrade, not a prerequisite; the concierge answers today without it
  • Crash safety in the composerA focused text field surviving a screen closing tripped an unguarded null inside React Native's scroll-view focus search and crashed the app natively · found, fixed and applied across every screen carrying a text field, this composer included · verified on a real device
  • DictationWORKING and verified on the device — the mic records with the same audio stack as voice memos, then the take is transcribed server-side (Whisper large v3 turbo on Cloudflare Workers AI) and lands in the composer to check and send · two 1 Aug fixes: a take silently cancelling itself, and the microphone dying app-wide after one recording (a library defect, patched at source with a self-healing retry)
